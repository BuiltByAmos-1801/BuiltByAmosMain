Features

Planned Capabilities

Discover the features we are building to help local shops connect with nearby customers digitally.

1. Business Discovery

Find and discover local businesses easily in your immediate neighborhood. (Planned)

2. Smart Search

Search specific businesses, active menus, and retail categories quickly. (Planned)

3. Business Profiles

Explore detailed store timings, photos, inventories, and contact information. (Planned)

4. Reviews & Ratings

Discover verified local businesses through honest customer feedback and recommendations. (Planned)

5. Favorites

Save your frequently visited neighborhood stores and businesses for quick access. (Planned)

6. Shop Owner Dashboard

Give business owners a central dashboard to update catalogs, offers, and store hours. (Planned)

7. Business Verification

Support verified profiles with identity verification to build consumer trust. (Planned)

8. Premium Listings

Future premium options giving active businesses highlighted visibility in search listings. (Planned)
