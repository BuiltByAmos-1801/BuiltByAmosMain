Google Business Profile Setup for Restaurants & Gyms

Your Google Business Profile (GBP) is often the first thing customers see. Here's how Ranchi restaurants and gyms can optimize it.

Choose the Right Category

Restaurants: "Restaurant" + cuisine type. Gyms: "Gym" or "Fitness center." Avoid vague categories — specificity helps rankings.

Add High-Quality Photos

Upload exterior, interior, menu/equipment, and team photos weekly. Businesses with photos get significantly more direction requests.

Write a Keyword-Rich Description

Mention your location (Ranchi), services, and what makes you unique. Example: "Family restaurant in Ranchi serving North Indian cuisine with dine-in and delivery."

Use Google Posts Weekly

Share offers, new menu items, gym batches, or events. Posts keep your profile active and improve engagement.

Collect & Respond to Reviews

Send WhatsApp links asking satisfied customers to review. Always reply — thank positive reviews and address concerns on negative ones.
