Local SEO: How to Rank on Google Maps in Ranchi

Local SEO helps your business appear when Ranchi customers search "near me." Follow these steps to improve Google Maps rankings.

Step 1: Claim Google Business Profile

Verify your business on Google. Use exact name, address, and phone (NAP). Choose the most accurate category — e.g., "Tattoo shop" or "Gym."

Step 2: Complete Every Section

Add photos, services, business hours, description with keywords like "Ranchi" and "Jharkhand," and post weekly updates.

Step 3: Get Google Reviews

Ask happy customers for reviews. Respond to all reviews professionally. Reviews are a top ranking factor for local search.

Step 4: Build a Mobile-Friendly Website

Link your website to GBP. Use local keywords in titles and headings. Our local SEO service handles this end-to-end.

Step 5: Local Citations

List your business consistently on Justdial, IndiaMART, and relevant directories with the same NAP details.
