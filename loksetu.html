The Challenge

Your Local Problems Deserve to Be Heard.

Reporting a problem is often easy to start but difficult to track, verify and follow through.

Broken Roads

Potholes, open trenches, and broken streets that disrupt daily commutes and affect safety.

Garbage & Waste

Unattended dumping yards, overflowing neighborhood bins, and unsanitary garbage accumulation.

Water Issues

Burst distribution pipes, polluted water supply, clogged drains, and water logging during rains.

Street Lights

Non-functioning street lamps creating dark patches on roads, impacting safety and visibility.

Traffic Issues

Broken traffic lights, hazardous bottlenecks, missing road dividers, and poor signage.

Public Infrastructure

Damaged community footpaths, broken park benches, and safety hazards in public buildings.

Environmental Issues

Unauthorized tree cutting, chemical dumping in local water bodies, and air/noise pollution hotspots.

Other Civic Problems

Any local issues that impact the quality of life and safety in your immediate neighborhood.

Workflow

How It Works

LokSetu is designed to make civic issues more visible, organized and trackable for everyone.

01

Report

Citizens can report local issues using photos/videos, detailed descriptions, and GPS location information.

02

Community

Nearby citizens can discover, support, upvote, and validate the reported issues to verify their genuineness.

03

Track

Follow the issue's lifecycle with an interactive timeline, receiving real-time status updates from start to finish.

04

Action

When responsible authorities or local organizations are active on the platform, issues can be routed directly to them.

Features

Search & Filters

Mobile First
